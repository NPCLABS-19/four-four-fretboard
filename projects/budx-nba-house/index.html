The experience

A global property.
An Indian first.

India’s first BUDX NBA House brought basketball, music and culture into one shared space. NBA, Budweiser and District came together for a two-day brand experience.

4/4 led end-to-end production, from creative development and showrunning to the details of on-ground execution.
